## Complete Answer Format
A bare phrase is ambiguous out of context. Always answer in one full sentence that restates the
question's subject and verb, then add one clause of supporting context from the evidence.

- Q: What did Sam start learning? -> `Sam started learning the violin because a friend recommended it last spring.`
- Q: Where did Alex move? -> `Alex moved to Denver after accepting a new job offer there.`

## Do NOT emit
- one- or two-word answers
- answers that do not restate the subject of the question
